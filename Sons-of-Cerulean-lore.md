Planet Cerulean

Location: In the Orpheus Sector and close to Vengeance

History:

The Old Ones and the Aeldari

- Originally was created by the Old Ones as a research hub to help defeat the Necrontyr and their C’tan. The planet was named *makil*, an Eldar word meaning “cut, hew with a sharp edge, kill, slay, forge metal”.   
- The planet itself was constructed from a core of wraithbone surrounded by an advanced reflective field. The core was a secret, experimental research hub created to design and test weaponry against the Necrontyr. Water was brought in to surround the core and create a protective barrier around the core from nearby planets. The high volume of water obfuscates the core and the pressure prevents only the most prudent and advanced to be able to get to it, should they even find out about its existence.  
- The Eldar created land formations on the water and filled it with genetically-made, dangerous organisms. This was done to both emulate a real planet and make it a hostile deterrent to those who might be curious.   
- The land formations were gigantic and looked like modern-day continents, but were actually floating islands. Each island held hundreds of thousands of square kilometers of defenses underground, supplementing the unnatural and dangerous fauna and wildlife.  
- Within the underground base were defense fortifications built within the continents to help fortify against invaders and house the Eldar. These fortifications were enormous and spanned the entirety of the underground bases. Not long afterwards, the Eldar built webway portals within the bases to travel between their kin across the Galaxy and provide the fruits of some of their research against the Necrontyr.   
- During the next few thousand years, the Eldar on Cerulean became obsessed with their research into better technologies to not only fight their enemies but also elevate themselves. In this sense, they became trapped like so many of their hedonistic kin, not caring about moral consequences of their actions, but only with their own technical advancements.   
- During the Fall of the Eldar, the psychic resonance caused most of the Eldar on the planet to die.  
- Afraid of losing their precious research and technology, the survivors on Makil destroyed their own warp gates to prevent any incursions onto their precious world. Considering the secrecy that was kept about the world, even amongst their own kin, Makil became isolated from the now shattered Aeldari empire.  
- Almost immediately after the destruction of the warp gates, the Aeldari scientists went back to their research. Now fixated even more now on developing weapons to avenge their fallen comrades and driven by hate against the Necrontyr.


The Golden Age of Mankind

- During the 22nd millennium, mankind had explored and colonized the Orpheus sector. Those few brave, foolhardy, or curious enough became interested in the planet’s unique biology.   
- The human colonists named the planet “Cerulean”, for its large, pristine blue oceans.  
- Despite the incredibly dangerous wildlife, the human colonies grew due to their highly advanced technologies, though there were and continues to have been a great deal of souls that have perished due to the poor conditions.  
- Eventually, the scientists and their AI were able to discover the enormous structures below the surface and start peeling away at the secrets of the mysterious planet.  
- However, even the AI weren't able to penetrate the shield surrounding the enormous core. Unable to penetrate the core and not willing to risk destroying the planet by using high energy weapons against it, the ancient humans and AI decided to continue probing the shields and analyze it using the data found in the Aeldari archives.  
- Unfortunately, during the dark age of mankind, the planet's population nearly died out from the war against the Men of Iron, only being saved from extinction by the ancient devices of the Old Ones and Eldar. With only a few handful of survivors remaining and not willing to deal with the horror of the aftermath, the survivors destroyed any trace of themselves from the surface and put themselves into some of the cryogenic freeze compartments. They would stay frozen in those compartments until other humans would arrive many thousands of years later.  
- During the Great Crusade, the planet was found by Rogue Trader Orpheus.  
- Initial scans of the planet showed unreadable data due to etheric storms. After many probing attempts, teams were eventually able to make planetfall via rare calm points in the etheric storms. Once the calm points could be calculated, it was only a matter of time that scouts were eventually able to determine more about the world. It was only after a few decades that, by chance, a scout patrol found an accessway with human readings.   
- The planet was found to house a large number of alien artifacts collected by the initial human inhabitants, alongside a treasure trove of data. There were many secrets that could possibly help the Imperium during the Great Crusade.  
- Orpheus kept the planet as secret as possible, only communicating highly encrypted messages to Malcador for aid in securing the planet and researching the underlying artifacts.  
- Malcador realized the importance of the mission and requested a company from every chapter; a retinue of Custodian Guard; a frigate of research teams; and a full complement of Mechanicus research vessels to the world of Cerulean. No one was to know of the reasons why they were chosen, nor where they were going. Only the head Navigators were given coordinates and even those were changed to the real directions upon arriving at their destinations. The only information given to the different elements were that they required the most inquisitive and tech abled minds. A unique request, but Malcador saw the importance of the artifacts. Additionally, Malcador felt that the difference in thinking from each Space Marine chapter could yield the best results from investigating the old world and its system. As more data streamed in, even the Emperor became intrigued about an intact Webway gate near a core of an enclosed, but seemingly abandoned city surrounded by a planet of water. It seems like the secrets of a true series of Imperial portals could be gathered from the study of the planet.  
- 18 companies of different sizes, but generally around 50-100 troops, each landed in system, along with entire Garrisons of Imperial Guard, Adeptus Mechanicus; Elite Troopers; and a even an Imperial Knights house to help secure and protect the valuable Intel and artifacts on Cerulean. Each of the elements were brought in from various paths and times, in order to obfuscate the formation of such a large force.   
- Orpheus went back to Vengeance and the newly founded Orpheus system, but kept the Cerulean system under close secrecy. No one was to know of the secrets of the planet except for Malcador and the Emperor.  
- In order to further keep the secrecy, space stations were hidden on the surrounding moons and cities were built within the underground bases, camouflaged using local fauna and vox scramblers.   
- All communication to and from the planet was purposefully cut off in order to keep the location as secure as possible. The sector was on the fringes of the Galaxy and reinforcements would take long to arrive.  
- Over the years, investigations were made into the planet's lost cities and many artifacts and technologies were discovered. Astartes legions came up with their own investigations, as delegated by the ruling Custodes. The combination of the some of the most curious minds in the Imperium lead to some breakthrough technologies. Then, came the Horus Heresy.


The Horus Heresy

- During the heresy, much of the planet was largely unaffected since word about the betrayal had not reached the planet. Information was strictly limited into and out of the system.   
- Only the small retinue of Custodian Guard knew about the Heresy and they were sworn to secrecy. The population didn't seem to mind the radio silence across the system, considering the wealth of information that existed on the planet and because of the nature of the ones chosen for their duties. Those that did raise concerns were reminded by the authorities of their duty and the importance of what they were doing. 

Post Heresy

- Several hundred years pass and the world becomes a thriving research hub. Technologies are found that rival or go past the Golden Age of humanity. Entire libraries of DataSlates are created.  
- Space Marine chapters have become increasingly integrated as older Marines die out and replaced by local populace. Company Captains of different legions begin integrating under a single Cerulean banner, forming the Sons of Cerulean legion.   
- Space Marine houses from each of the original 18 chapters still exist. Each is a training ground and recruitment for initiates daring enough to become Astartes. The houses are formed in a circle surrounding inner-most citadel on the Imperium-held continent.  
- Citadel: A fortress bastion that was built to reflect some of the glory on Terra. It now houses the administration and Imperial power on Cerulean. The council of power meet at the center of the citadel, with the ever present Custodian, Hesaar, at the very head.  
- Mechanicus research has led to drastic improvements in augmetics and weapons systems. Nano metals research leads to creation of near-living cities within the crust.  
- For the human population, food and water generation have been automated due to finding lost agricultural technologies. Generations of scientists also live longer, healthier, and more appreciative lives.  
- Imperium exists mostly only within a single continent within Cerulean. Expeditionary missions to other areas have been fraught with danger, due to a combination of etheric storms; extremely hostile creatures within the oceans/other land masses; and volatile landscapes that seem to only breed danger. Still, the Imperium has tried to scout as much of the world within the last 10,000 years since its inception on the planet and the Imperium does have a general map of the world. The Alpha Legion and Ravenguard houses have been especially adept at training operatives to gather data within the furthest areas of the planet, but Custodian Hesaar is always suspicious that the Alpha Legion operatives are always withholding some of that knowledge.  
- Frustrated with the difficulty of expanding outwards, the Imperium has instead focused downwards towards the tunnels that were apparently explored by humans many millenia ago.   
- Imperium explorers eventually find the cryogenic chambers of the ancient humans.

Old Humans

- Upon finding the old humans, the DNA activators get to work, awakening the ancient dwellers from their stasis.  
- After 6 months of recovery, the first ancient, Syl, recalls the horrors brought on by the Men of Iron and describes their own understanding of the ancient world. The newer inhabitants of Cerulean find out the true nature of the world and its  ancient beginnings.

Current Day

- Legion Master Strone, Custodian Captain Hesaar, Arch Magos Tarketh, and Imperial Commander Versus lay down plans to breach the core of the world and attain the resources.  
- Syl and the ancient humans attempt to help the current humans with their research duties and find meaning in helping the newer humans understand the planet and its technologies.  
- Cerulean command decides to send 3 drones towards the core and fit them to send live feeds of data back. 200 miles into the journey and the feed cuts abruptly and all communication is lost with the drones all at once.  
- Cerulean command doesn't understand what's happening and consults Syl and his group. Syl doesn't understand either and asks to be put into an expeditionary group trailing behind another set of drones to see what happens to them. Piloting one of the drones from a visible distance away, Syl watches in horror as a beast incomprehensible in size destroys the drones again with no effort and then disappears back into the depth. Syl feels the horror he had during the time of the Men of Iron.  
- Cerulean Command looks at the data feed from Syl's vessel and determines that a coordinated attack force is needed to destroy the beast. Mechanicus, Imperial Navy, and Space Marine vessels are sent down to the depths in order to destroy the monster and gain access to the core.   
- Probes are launched simultaneously from all the vessels to bring out the Leviathan. Leviathan comes out, but the data doesn't do the monster justice. A beast seemingly the size of an Imperial frigate appears and uses its sheer size to destroy the probes. All vessels fire weapons at the enemy. Macro cannons, Melta-Torpedos, Lance Beams, and various other munitions are fired within less than a few miles of the Leviathan, but seem to only cause damage to its superficial layers.  
- Alerted of the imposing threat, the Leviathan turns its maws on the Imperial fleet and rushes into it. Evasive maneuvers barely save the vessels from certain death while the ships continue firing into the creature with little effect.  
- Desperate for a solution, the Arch Magos reveals a prototype weapon created from its research labs. The Mechanicus loaders whisper	 their prayers into the machine spirit of the prototype weapon, a large, boring machine with a reactor that can potentially rip time and space.  
- Warning the other ships, the Mechanicus fire the weapon directly into the side of the creature. The beast hardly notices and comes around towards the Imperial Navy Vessels as the melta cutters desperately attempt to break through the scaled skin.   
- Unable to break through the skin, the Magos aboard decides to pull all ships to the surface as quickly as possible and detonate the device. Realizing that the ships won't get to the surface fast enough, a Sons of Cerulean Space Marine vessel, Angron's Rage,  intervenes and charges towards the creature, launches all of its remaining weapons, and then evades towards the core.  
- Sensing the ship attempting to reach further down the core, the Leviathan turns about and follows the Space Marine vessel downwards. Once at a safe enough distance, the trigger is pulled and the device activates, immediately ripping the creature in two and creating a mile-wide hole in the middle of the deep. The broken creature is sucked into the hole, along with the Space Marine Vessel.  
- All vessels return to the command center in Cerulean Sector VIV for debriefing. Legion Master Strone is furious at the loss of 20 good Space Marines and the accompanying ship. Arch Magos Tarketh is questioned by the Cerulean Council about the device used in the explosion. For now, it seems that the creature and the Space Marine vessel are lost, but the path to the Core seems clear, for now.  
- Drones are sent once more down towards the core and reach the outer shield. The shield makes the core appear like a star, burning bright white and thrumming with energy. Drones analyze the shield

Additional details:

- There are many continents, each composed of completely different biodiversity, due to the isolation of the islands from one another. The isolation is due to the extreme weather environments and other dangers between continents.  
- There are also a lot of different geologic and weather patterns across each continent. The creatures and life forms across each island continent have adapted to the environment due to Eldar Gene manipulation many millennia back. The death World contains many creatures, insects, plants, each would high levels of resilience, toxicity, and deadliness.   
- Since the arrival of humans in the 22nd millennium, the native bioforms have also adapted to hunting and killing humans. The memory of the genetic makeup of humans is stored in the bioform genes. Researchers have found that they are not unlike the tyranid creatures that they have read up on from data slates coming from the new Imperium.  
- Due to its isolation, the Imperial citizens on Cerulean do not worship the emperor, but most do hold loyalty to the ideals of the Imperium and to the emperor. The older human tribes are less inclined to believe in the Imperium and hold mostly to their old traditions. However, 10,000 years of isolation has led to many feeling more attached to their homeworld than to the Imperium at large.  
- 10,000 years of secrecy and research has led to both a culture of intimacy within the planet, but also a curiosity of things outside.

Eldar Science and Lore:

- The concept of mimic creatures that were modified from old Eldar. They were made to hide from their foes, but have become depraved and psychotic, obsessed with hunting in the depths of their old labs.   
- The old labs are the kilometers of architecture hidden beneath the floating islands. These are seemingly endless. Once containing hordes of Eldar scientists and engineers, they are dark places, some still dimly lit. They are haunted by the remnants of the Eldar creations and some of the Eldar themselves, depraved and mutated to survive. There are only a few of the original, sane Eldar left within frozen containers, secured until reinforcements arrive for rescue.
